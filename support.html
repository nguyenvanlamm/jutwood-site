How to play

Tap to drop the sliding plank. Each plank rests on the one below, and the stack holds as long as the weight above every plank still sits over it; lean too far and everything above tips into the river. Pass the stakes for stars and tap Done to keep your reach. In Rapids, reach the far bank. Rope takes back the last drop, a Spare Plank adds one more, and the Plumb Line shows where each layer balances.

Purchases

Creek and Free Reach are free. Far Banks ($3.99, once) opens Millpond and Rapids; a Lumber Cart ($0.99) adds 4 Rope, 3 Spare Plank and 3 Plumb Line. Bought something before, or on another device with the same store account? Open Settings → Restore purchases.

Progress

Progress is saved on the device only. It is not synced between devices, and it is removed if you delete the app.
