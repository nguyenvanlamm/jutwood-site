Privacy Policy

Jutwood · last updated 2 November 2026

Jutwood is a balance game: beavers stack planks out over a river. It does not ask who you are, and it does not sell any data about you.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress: the stars you earned on each level, your best reach in Free Reach, and how many Rope, Spare Plank and Plumb Line tools you have.
  • Whether you have bought Far Banks, and which purchases were already delivered, so none is granted twice.

This is stored on your device only. It is not synced or sent anywhere. Deleting the app removes it.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Purchases

Chapter 1 (Creek) and Free Reach are free. Purchases (Far Banks and the Lumber Cart) are handled entirely by the App Store under its own privacy policy. We receive no payment details; the game only learns from the store which purchases exist.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
